What Illinois requires

Every firearm sale and pawn in Illinois goes through state rules. You'll need a valid Illinois FOID card and a government-issued photo ID, and purchases include a background check and a state waiting period.

Firearm questions

Can I sell a gun to a pawn shop in Springfield, IL?

Yes. Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop is a licensed firearms dealer and buys handguns, rifles and shotguns. Bring a valid photo ID and your Illinois FOID card.

Can I pawn a firearm?

Yes. You can take a pawn loan on a firearm and pick it up when you repay. You'll need a valid FOID card both when you pawn it and when you pick it up, and you'll go through a background check to get it back.

Do you do FFL transfers?

Yes. Call (217) 414-0909 before you order a firearm online so we can confirm the details and send our license information to the seller.

How should I bring a firearm into the store?

Unloaded and in a case. Tell staff you're bringing in a firearm when you arrive.

Is there a waiting period to buy a gun in Illinois?

Yes. Illinois law requires a waiting period after purchase before you can take a firearm home, along with a FOID card and a background check. We'll explain the timing when you buy.

Tell us the make, model and condition and we'll get back to you to set up a time to see it.
