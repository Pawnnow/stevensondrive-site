What we buy

Not sure if we'll take it? Call or send a quote request. Firearms have their own page.

Gold and silver

Broken chains, single earrings, class rings, dental gold and scrap. We test the karat and weigh it in front of you, and pay based on the metal.

Jewelry and diamonds

Rings, necklaces, bracelets and watches. Diamonds and gemstones can add value beyond the metal, so bring any certificates or receipts you have.

Coins and bullion

Silver and gold coins, bars and rounds, plus collectible coins. Leave them in their holders; cleaning coins can lower their value.

Power tools

Cordless drills, impact drivers, saws and tool sets from brands like DeWalt, Milwaukee and Makita. Batteries and chargers make a big difference.

Electronics

Laptops, tablets, TVs, speakers and cameras. Bring chargers and remotes, and sign out of your accounts before you come in.

Game consoles

PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo consoles, controllers and games. Consoles should power on and be signed out of your account.

Musical instruments

Guitars, amplifiers, keyboards and band instruments. Cases and accessories help the offer.

Sporting goods

Bows, fishing gear, golf clubs, exercise equipment and other outdoor gear in good working shape.

Selling questions

Is it better to sell or pawn my item?

Sell if you don't need the item back; you'll usually get more cash. Pawn if you want to keep it; you get a loan now and pick the item up when you repay.

Where can I sell gold in Springfield, IL?

Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop buys gold, silver and jewelry at 2223 Adlai Stevenson Drive. We test and weigh your gold in front of you and pay the same visit.

Do I need ID to sell to a pawn shop?

Yes. We need a valid government-issued photo ID for every sale. Firearms also require a valid Illinois FOID card.

Can I get a quote before I come in?

Yes. Send a quote request with the brand, model and condition, or call (217) 414-0909. For gold and jewelry, a final offer needs to be made in person after testing.
