Repairs we handle in the store

Ring sizing

Make a ring larger or smaller so it fits comfortably and stays put, including rings with stones.

Chain and clasp repair

Fix broken links, replace worn jump rings, and swap out clasps that won't stay closed.

Soldering

Rejoin broken bands, bracelets and charms with a clean, strong solder joint.

Prong and stone work

Tighten loose stones and rebuild worn prongs before a diamond or gemstone falls out.

Cleaning and polishing

Bring back the shine on rings, chains and bracelets that have gone dull with everyday wear.

Watch batteries

Battery replacement for most watches, done at the counter.

How a repair works

  1. Bring it in

    Stop by during store hours with the piece. No appointment needed.

  2. Get a price and time

    We look it over, explain what it needs, and give you a price and a time estimate before any work starts.

  3. Pick it up

    We call when it's ready. Pick it up at the same counter you dropped it off.

Why repair it locally

Many stores send repairs out to a shop in another city. That means extra days, extra handling, and no one at the counter who has actually seen the work.

At Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop the repair is done here. Your jewelry stays in the building, and the person who quoted the job is the one who does it.

Jewelry repair questions

How long does a jewelry repair take?

It depends on the repair. When you drop a piece off, we'll look it over and give you a price and a time estimate before any work starts.

Do you repair jewelry that wasn't bought here?

Yes. Bring in rings, chains, bracelets and other pieces from anywhere, and we'll tell you what it needs and what it costs.

Can you resize a ring in Springfield while I wait?

Some simple jobs can be done quickly, but most sizing is a drop-off. Call ahead at (217) 414-0909 and we'll tell you what to expect for your ring.

Can you fix a broken chain or clasp?

Yes. Broken links, worn jump rings and failed clasps are some of the most common repairs we do.

Do you replace watch batteries?

Yes. Bring your watch in and we'll replace the battery at the counter.

Have a piece that needs fixing?

Bring it by the store or send us a description and we'll tell you what to expect.
