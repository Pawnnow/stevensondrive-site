About Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop

A pawn shop and jewelry repair counter on Adlai Stevenson Drive in Springfield, Illinois.

What we do

Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop helps Springfield get cash for what they own, whether that's a short-term pawn loan or a straight sale. We buy and sell gold, jewelry, coins, tools, electronics and more, and we're a licensed firearms dealer.

We also repair jewelry in the store. Ring sizing, chain repair, soldering and stone work are done here, not shipped out.
