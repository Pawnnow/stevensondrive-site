Frequently asked questions

Answers about pawn loans, selling, firearms and jewelry repair at Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop. Don't see your question? Call (217) 414-0909.

General

How does a pawn loan work in Springfield, IL?

You bring in an item of value and we loan you money against it. We hold the item while the loan is open. When you repay the loan plus the finance charge, you get your item back. If you choose not to repay, the item becomes ours and you owe nothing more.

What do I need to bring?

Bring the item and a valid government-issued photo ID. For firearms, Illinois law also requires a valid FOID card. Chargers, cases, boxes and paperwork can raise what we're able to offer.

Does a pawn loan affect my credit?

No. We don't run a credit check, and the loan is secured by your item, so nothing is reported to credit bureaus.

Can I sell or pawn a gun at Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop?

Yes. We're a licensed firearms dealer and we buy, sell and loan on handguns, rifles and shotguns. You'll need a valid photo ID and an Illinois FOID card. Please bring firearms in unloaded and cased.

Do you repair jewelry in the store?

Yes. Ring sizing, chain repair, soldering and other repairs are done here at the store, so your jewelry doesn't get shipped out. Bring it in for a quote.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in any time during business hours. If you'd like an idea of value first, send a quote request or call (217) 414-0909.

Pawn loans and selling

What happens if I can't repay my pawn loan?

If you don't repay, the item becomes ours and the loan is closed. There are no collection calls and nothing goes on your credit. Ask us about extending the loan if you need more time.

How much can I borrow?

It depends on the item's resale value and condition. Bring it in or send a quote request and we'll give you a number.

Is it better to sell or pawn my item?

Sell if you don't need the item back; you'll usually get more cash. Pawn if you want to keep it; you get a loan now and pick the item up when you repay.

Firearms

Do you do FFL transfers?

Yes. Call (217) 414-0909 before you order a firearm online so we can confirm the details and send our license information to the seller.

Is there a waiting period to buy a gun in Illinois?

Yes. Illinois law requires a waiting period after purchase before you can take a firearm home, along with a FOID card and a background check. We'll explain the timing when you buy.

Jewelry repair

How long does a jewelry repair take?

It depends on the repair. When you drop a piece off, we'll look it over and give you a price and a time estimate before any work starts.

Do you repair jewelry that wasn't bought here?

Yes. Bring in rings, chains, bracelets and other pieces from anywhere, and we'll tell you what it needs and what it costs.

Not sure what it's worth?

Send us a few details and we'll get back to you with an offer.
