Questions people ask us

How does a pawn loan work in Springfield, IL?

You bring in an item of value and we loan you money against it. We hold the item while the loan is open. When you repay the loan plus the finance charge, you get your item back. If you choose not to repay, the item becomes ours and you owe nothing more.

What do I need to bring?

Bring the item and a valid government-issued photo ID. For firearms, Illinois law also requires a valid FOID card. Chargers, cases, boxes and paperwork can raise what we're able to offer.

Does a pawn loan affect my credit?

No. We don't run a credit check, and the loan is secured by your item, so nothing is reported to credit bureaus.

Can I sell or pawn a gun at Stevenson Drive Pawn Shop?

Yes. We're a licensed firearms dealer and we buy, sell and loan on handguns, rifles and shotguns. You'll need a valid photo ID and an Illinois FOID card. Please bring firearms in unloaded and cased.

Do you repair jewelry in the store?

Yes. Ring sizing, chain repair, soldering and other repairs are done here at the store, so your jewelry doesn't get shipped out. Bring it in for a quote.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in any time during business hours. If you'd like an idea of value first, send a quote request or call (217) 414-0909.
