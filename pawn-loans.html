How a pawn loan works

  1. Bring in your item

    Stop by with the item and a valid photo ID. Chargers, cases and paperwork help.

  2. Get an offer

    We look it over and tell you how much we can loan. You decide, with no pressure either way.

  3. Repay and pick it up

    Leave with cash. Repay the loan plus the finance charge by the due date and your item comes home.

What you can pawn

We loan on items with a clear resale value. The most common are gold and jewelry, firearms, power tools, electronics, game consoles and musical instruments.

Working condition and completeness matter. A drill with its battery and charger, or a console with its controller, will get a better offer than the item alone.

What to bring

  • The item, cleaned up if you can
  • Chargers, cases, boxes and paperwork
  • A valid government-issued photo ID
  • For firearms, your Illinois FOID card

Loan terms

Pawn loans in Illinois are regulated by the state. We'll walk you through the loan length, the finance charge and your due date before you sign, and everything is printed on your pawn ticket.

No credit check

No collections

If you choose not to repay, you keep the cash and we keep the item. That's the end of it.

Your item is stored safely

We hold pawned items securely in the store until you come back for them.

Pawn loan questions

What is a pawn loan?

A pawn loan is a short-term loan secured by something you own. We hold the item while the loan is open, and you get it back when you repay the loan plus the finance charge.

Does a pawn loan affect my credit?

No. We don't run a credit check, and nothing is reported to credit bureaus. The item is the only security for the loan.

What happens if I can't repay my pawn loan?

If you don't repay, the item becomes ours and the loan is closed. There are no collection calls and nothing goes on your credit. Ask us about extending the loan if you need more time.

How much can I borrow?

It depends on the item's resale value and condition. Bring it in or send a quote request and we'll give you a number.

What do I need to get a pawn loan in Springfield, IL?

Bring the item and a valid government-issued photo ID. Firearms also require a valid Illinois FOID card.
